- **Sign wall** (DONE, tested 2026-11-15; planned for 1.4.0, decided 2026-11-13): a fence that shows a sign design repeated along its whole length, following its curve. Its own fence object, one side only (the road side), no material tab and no swatch / colour options: the design is its only look. First version 4 m tall, using the 4 m sign's size and layouts (read from the game's sign type at run time, so the proportions and the repeat length match); a 1 m tall wall with the 1 m sign later.
  How: the game's widget signs draw their design (FPrefabSignData) into a render target through AFGSignSubsystem and show it on a plane with a material instance (`AFGBuildableWidgetSign::SetupMaterialInstanceForProxyPlane` is virtual). The wall owns a hidden companion sign (subclass of AFGBuildableWidgetSign: no mesh, no collision, not in the build menu) standing inside the wall at the middle of its length, its significance range raised to half the wall's length plus the normal sign range so the design shows anywhere along it. The wall puts the companion's render target on its own mesh with UVs repeating every (height x the sign's aspect). Saved reference wall -> companion. E on the wall opens the game's sign editor for the companion; copy / paste settings and middle click copy the design; multiplayer and saving come from the game's sign. Dismantle takes the companion along (free, nothing to refund); Cut / Join give each new piece its own companion with a copy of the design. Check blueprints carry both.
  Limits: fixed sign sizes (the game's editor has no free proportions); the design is as sharp as the game's sign texture (soft right next to a tall wall); far away, like signs, no design (plain colour).
  - Built (step 1): ERMFenceType::SignWall, `ARMBuildableSignWall` (fence wall subclass; `Build_FenceWall_Sign`, walls menu, Road Maker milestone, 2 iron plates + 2 wire per 4 m). Shape: the concrete fence wall (4 m x 0.5 m) with 2 cm bevels (`RMGeometry::BuildSignWall`); front face `BuildSignWallFace` (UV1.x = design widths along, wrapped; v top to bottom) with `M_RM_SignWallFront` (texture param SignTexture, frac UV, a little glow); body `M_RM_SignWallMetal` (grid fence metal); back strip `M_RM_SignWallBack` (metal + CIRCUIT_HLSL grooves, bump). Hidden sign `ARMSignWallSign` (C++ child of AFGBuildableWidgetSign) takes the settings of Build_StandaloneWidgetSign_Large (8 x 4 m) at construction by reflection (sign + sign base EditDefaultsOnly properties, interact widget, plane mesh); plane tiny with big bounds, no collision; its SetupMaterialInstanceForProxyPlane hands the render target to the wall (and logs the design material parameters). Wall makes the sign 0.5 s after BeginPlay (server, if none saved), E / look-at text forwarded to it, sign in the dismantle children, destroyed with the wall. Build.cs: OnlineIntegration (sign header).
  - Fixes after testing (2026-11-15): the face uses the sign's own design material (MID from the hidden sign's plane: background, animation, glow like the game sign), UV0 0..1 per 8 m design, SignWidth / SignHeight set to the real size; the hidden sign gets the wall before FinishSpawning (no white first frame); text reads the right way on both sides; a 0.2 s check re-applies the design after a paste and marks the hidden sign "seen" while any part of the wall is drawn (the game blurs designs of signs off screen); same resolution as the 8 x 4 small billboard; the design shows at every distance level (was gone past ~200 m). - **GPU-drawn map roads** (DONE, tested 2026-11-15, also with a map zoom mod beyond 8x: markings on small roads appear as expected; planned for 1.4.0, decided 2026-11-15; after issue #4, modelled on FactoryAtlas's map painter): replace the 512 m picture tiles (CPU-painted, 1 px = 1 m, ~1.3 MB each with mips: 130-260 MB for a network across much of the map, blurry at high zoom, whole tiles repainted on a change) with shapes drawn by the GPU, as Atlas does: one 16-32 byte record per road piece (~4 m strip: position, half length / width, angle, colour / style) in a Slate instance buffer (ISlateUpdatableInstanceBuffer) drawn with a map material, a few draw calls per frame; pan / zoom change only the transform. Intersections (outlines that do not fit one record) as triangles in a small separate buffer. Records sorted by height so bridges still cover what is under them. Estimates for ~4000 roads (~100 m each): ~100,000 records, ~3 MB, near-zero CPU per frame (Atlas: ~0.3 ms per million buildings), light GPU work even on a handheld iGPU; buffers freed while the map is closed and rebuilt on opening. Best fit for low-VRAM handhelds (shared memory). Keep: our layer directly above mMap (issue #4 rule), "Show Roads on Map", road colours / paint.
  - **Detail when zoomed in** (other map mods allow ~8-10x more zoom): above a zoom threshold (the material gets pixels per metre), fade in the lane lines (dividers, dashes 3 m / 6 m gap, continuous, side lines, in the road's line colours; kept at least ~1 px wide) and, higher still, the lane markings (arrows, numbers, vehicle icons) as small textured quads from one icon atlas, drawn at their real size and turned with the lane. Same records as the strips (lanes count, line style) plus one record per marking.
  - Stopgap if memory is reported before then: free the tiles while the map is closed and repaint only the visible ones on opening.
  - Built (2026-11-15): `RMMapLayer.cpp` rewritten. Our widget (SRMMapRoadsLayer, an SLeafWidget painted every frame) sits where the tile canvas was (directly above mMap). One FVector4f record per ~4 m road piece in a Slate instance buffer (x, y map units; z angle 10 bits + half length 7 bits (5 cm) + half width 7 bits (25 cm); w fill / line palette indices + flags), drawn by the UI material `M_RM_MapRoads` (make_materials.py; WPO places the quad, Customized UVs to the pixel stage, wired by `URMEditorTools::ConnectCustomizedUV`); 256-colour palette texture. Pieces reach into their neighbours on bends (half width x tan(half turn) + 10 cm). Intersections: CPU polygons each frame (fan + 1 px soft rim, curb lines as quads) with a transient white texture brush. Draw order: 3 m height layers, inside a layer intersections, walking paths, roads, each piece whole. Side lines 2.5 m, at least 1.5 px. Records uploaded only when a piece changes.
  - Fixes after the first test: stripes where a walking path beside a road took turns with it (exact-height order → layers); 1 px gaps at every joint ("stairs", dashed side lines): the material returned P - corner, Slate does not add the vertex position.
  - Zoom levels (built and tested 2026-11-15), read from the slider (render scale of mMapZoomContainer; fallback: map size over the smallest seen; 1x = 0.13 px/m, 8x = 1 px/m on the user's screen): walking paths from 1.5x; side lines on roads 2 wide and up always, on walking paths from 3x, never on 1-wide roads; lane dividers (dashed 3 / 6 m with a per-piece dash phase, or continuous; middle-line colour, cyberpunk: secondary) from 6x (fade from 5x); intersection curb lines whenever the intersection has them. Record w = style (palette row of fill / side line / divider colours) + dash phase + flags (side lines, continuous, lanes, walking path, 2+ wide).
  - Lane markings on the map (built and tested 2026-11-15): arrows and digits as their shapes (BuildMarkingMesh, flat), vehicles as their icon textures; each shown once its length is 8 px on screen (fade over 4 px), so screen size and zoom mods count (a marking on a 4-wide 2-lane road, 12 m lane cap, shows at 8x); drawn after the roads of their height layer, at the height of their highest point + 1 m (steep roads covered them).
  - Closed 2026-11-15: performance fine on the handheld (GPD Win 4), no visible impact at 4K; buffers kept while the map is closed (a few MB, instant reopening; freeing them decided against); no bridge check (decided not to pursue a bridge feature).
- **Cyberpunk road on Low quality: circuit grooves back** (DONE, tested 2026-11-15; option 1 chosen). Today the Low branch of M_RoadCyber drops the procedural circuit (CIRCUIT_HLSL + bump_normal) and shows plain plastic with the glowing lines. Options (High is untouched by all of them, the QualitySwitch keeps the procedural version): (1) bake the circuit height into a repeating texture in make_materials.py (draw a bake material to a render target, save it as a texture; cell indices wrapped so it tiles every 48 x 8 m) and read it on Low for normal and groove colour; tracks are ~2 cm wide so it needs ~1 cm/px, ~4096 x 1024, ~4-5 MB, a cut track every 48 m; (2) a cheaper procedural Low: one layer of tracks, no rings or chips, about half the cost, no texture; (3) a small hand-made 512 px groove texture: very cheap but a different design. Suggested: (1).
  - Built: `Tools/IconSource/bake_cyber_circuit.py` ports CIRCUIT_HLSL to numpy and bakes a tiling height mask `Tools/Icons/T_RM_CyberCircuit.png`, 48 m along x 16 m across (16 m so wide roads repeat a strip at most twice), 2048 x 1024 (2.3 x 1.6 cm per px), DXT1 ~1.3 MB with mips (create_assets.py: linear, wrap). To tile, every cell grid divides the tile (second layer 2.4 m sections, 8 m density cells) and every hash reads its cell index wrapped; the second layer fills the first one's empty patches (no bare squares repeating every 48 m); tracks stop crisply around chips. M_RoadCyber Low branch: the mask (UV = metres along / across / (48, 16)) for the groove colour and, through bump_normal(low_height), the bump. High unchanged.
  - Sizes compared 2026-11-15: 4096 x 2048 (~5.3 MB, sharpest), 2048 x 2048 (~2.7 MB, kept in `Tools/IconSource/Baked/` in case it is needed), 2048 x 1024 chosen.

- **Integration with other mods / Automated Machines** (discussed 2026-11-12, postponed): no hard link between mods; a
  name-based contract through Unreal reflection (works in the shipped game, where metadata is stripped). An actor is
  "automatable" when it has `AM_GetAlarms()`, `AM_GetActions()`, `AM_DoAction(Action, On)` and an `AM_OnAlarm(Alarm)`
  event; Automated Machines finds them by name and lists them like a machine's alarms / actions. Road Maker candidates:
  intersection "Vehicle enters / leaves", "Busy / Clear" (the detection traffic lights need), road "Vehicle on road /
  Road clear", boom barrier actions "Open / Closed" + alarms, traffic light "Turns green / red" + "Hold red". Optional
  extra: a Road Maker subsystem publishing world events (road built / dismantled / cut / split / merged / joined,
  intersection built) for other mods. Open questions: `AM_` naming as the shared standard; which vehicles count.
