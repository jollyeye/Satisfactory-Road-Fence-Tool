# Road Maker 1.4.0 roadmap

1.3.0 released 2026-11-14; 1.3.1 (issue #3 fixes) being finished in [ROADMAP-1.3.0.md](ROADMAP-1.3.0.md). Items moved here from its "Later" section on 2026-11-15.
Status words: **DONE** (tested in game), **built, awaiting test**, otherwise to do.

## Done
- **Sign wall** (DONE, tested 2026-11-15; planned for 1.4.0, decided 2026-11-13): a fence that shows a sign design repeated along its whole length, following its curve. Its own fence object, one side only (the road side), no material tab and no swatch / colour options: the design is its only look. First version 4 m tall, using the 4 m sign's size and layouts (read from the game's sign type at run time, so the proportions and the repeat length match); a 1 m tall wall with the 1 m sign later.
  How: the game's widget signs draw their design (FPrefabSignData) into a render target through AFGSignSubsystem and show it on a plane with a material instance (`AFGBuildableWidgetSign::SetupMaterialInstanceForProxyPlane` is virtual). The wall owns a hidden companion sign (subclass of AFGBuildableWidgetSign: no mesh, no collision, not in the build menu) standing inside the wall at the middle of its length, its significance range raised to half the wall's length plus the normal sign range so the design shows anywhere along it. The wall puts the companion's render target on its own mesh with UVs repeating every (height x the sign's aspect). Saved reference wall -> companion. E on the wall opens the game's sign editor for the companion; copy / paste settings and middle click copy the design; multiplayer and saving come from the game's sign. Dismantle takes the companion along (free, nothing to refund); Cut / Join give each new piece its own companion with a copy of the design. Check blueprints carry both.
  Limits: fixed sign sizes (the game's editor has no free proportions); the design is as sharp as the game's sign texture (soft right next to a tall wall); far away, like signs, no design (plain colour).
  - Built (step 1): ERMFenceType::SignWall, `ARMBuildableSignWall` (fence wall subclass; `Build_FenceWall_Sign`, walls menu, Road Maker milestone, 2 iron plates + 2 wire per 4 m). Shape: the concrete fence wall (4 m x 0.5 m) with 2 cm bevels (`RMGeometry::BuildSignWall`); front face `BuildSignWallFace` (UV1.x = design widths along, wrapped; v top to bottom) with `M_RM_SignWallFront` (texture param SignTexture, frac UV, a little glow); body `M_RM_SignWallMetal` (grid fence metal); back strip `M_RM_SignWallBack` (metal + CIRCUIT_HLSL grooves, bump). Hidden sign `ARMSignWallSign` (C++ child of AFGBuildableWidgetSign) takes the settings of Build_StandaloneWidgetSign_Large (8 x 4 m) at construction by reflection (sign + sign base EditDefaultsOnly properties, interact widget, plane mesh); plane tiny with big bounds, no collision; its SetupMaterialInstanceForProxyPlane hands the render target to the wall (and logs the design material parameters). Wall makes the sign 0.5 s after BeginPlay (server, if none saved), E / look-at text forwarded to it, sign in the dismantle children, destroyed with the wall. Build.cs: OnlineIntegration (sign header).
  - Fixes after testing (2026-11-15): the face uses the sign's own design material (MID from the hidden sign's plane: background, animation, glow like the game sign), UV0 0..1 per 8 m design, SignWidth / SignHeight set to the real size; the hidden sign gets the wall before FinishSpawning (no white first frame); text reads the right way on both sides; a 0.2 s check re-applies the design after a paste and marks the hidden sign "seen" while any part of the wall is drawn (the game blurs designs of signs off screen); same resolution as the 8 x 4 small billboard; the design shows at every distance level (was gone past ~200 m). Copy / paste between sign walls; middle click on a sign wall carries its design to the next walls built (30 s). Copying a sign wall onto a normal game sign is not supported (decided 2026-11-15).
  - Cut / Join (DONE, tested 2026-11-15): each new piece's sign gets the old wall's design (CopyCutSettingsTo takes the game's CopySettings of the old sign, EnsureSign pastes it; SetPrefabSignData is not exported by the game). After a cut the 8 m designs restart at each piece's start.
  - Build menu icon (DONE 2026-11-15): `render_icons.py fencewall_sign`, the fence wall with a metal body and a black face with orange "FICSIT".
  - Still open for the sign wall: blueprints, replacing walls.
- **Fences fill gaps** (DONE, tested 2026-11-15): snapped to a road edge that already has fences of the same family (any fence wall for a fence wall, the same road fence for a road fence), a new fence fills only the free stretch under the aim, end to end with its neighbours (`ARMFenceHologram::ClipEdgeToFreeGap`). E.g. cut a concrete wall, dismantle the middle piece and put a sign wall in the gap.
- **Fences turn round on a road edge** (DONE, tested 2026-11-15): with the snap key on a road edge or intersection curb, each scroll step flips the fence's facing (mInwardSign), like the street lights; kept while the hologram lives. Grid fences (Metal Grid Fence, Steel Mesh Fence Wall) centred on their line (were 3 cm off, jumped 6 cm when flipped).
- **Walking path** (DONE, tested in game 2026-11-15; planned for 1.4.0, decided 2026-11-14, renamed from "sidewalks" 2026-11-15; answer to GitHub issue #1, a 1.5-foundation road for trucks): no 1.5-wide road. Instead a walking path piece that snaps along a road's side like a road beside a road (whole length, edge to edge, same curve and slope), widening it: e.g. 2 m on each side of a 1-wide road = 12 m, 1.5 foundations. Flush with the road by default; for a raised sidewalk, nudge the hologram up (no separate sidewalk piece). Not a road: no lanes, lane lines, markings or path / track snapping on it; the road keeps all of that. Fences and fence walls snap to the walking path's outer edge like to a road edge (follow it, face the road). To decide: widths (2 m / 4 m?), material (concrete like the road top, paintable?), what happens on intersections (follow the curbs?), Cut / Join / Adjust Curvature carrying walking paths along.
  - Built as: `Build_WalkingPath_2m` / `_4m`, RMBuildableRoad children with `mWidthCm` 200 / 400, `mIsWalkingPath`, `mDefaultLook` 1 (Concrete Blocks); build menu Roads after the intersection. Lines: at most one middle line (2 lanes), no side lines (`LimitMarkings`). No vehicle path / track lane snapping (aimed path = ground; walking ends give no snap points), no lane markings, not merged or split; Join only path to path of the same width. Ends snap only to walking path ends (centred); roads not to path ends. Beside a road: the snap key, like a road beside a road. Intersections: picked like road ends (mixed or walking-only), to be tested.
  - Changes after the first test (2026-11-15): Split only on 4 m paths (two 2 m, split unit 2 m), Merge two 2 m paths into 4 m (a road and a path never merge); side lines on 4 m only (inset 30 cm); no lines by default; a 2 m path snaps to a 4 m path end left / middle / right; walking paths snap to road caps every 2 m (edge aligned) and keep their own look and lines there; build menu icons with a white walking man (4 m: continuous middle line, figure in the right lane).
  - Middle click on a road / walking path also copies its look and lines (RMRoadSample: noted on the Sample key, taken by the next road hologram of the same class within 3 s).

- **Cyberpunk road on Low quality: circuit grooves back** (decision for 1.4.0, raised 2026-11-15). Today the Low branch of M_RoadCyber drops the procedural circuit (CIRCUIT_HLSL + bump_normal) and shows plain plastic with the glowing lines. Options (High is untouched by all of them, the QualitySwitch keeps the procedural version): (1) bake the circuit height into a repeating texture in make_materials.py (draw a bake material to a render target, save it as a texture; cell indices wrapped so it tiles every 48 x 8 m) and read it on Low for normal and groove colour; tracks are ~2 cm wide so it needs ~1 cm/px, ~4096 x 1024, ~4-5 MB, a cut track every 48 m; (2) a cheaper procedural Low: one layer of tracks, no rings or chips, about half the cost, no texture; (3) a small hand-made 512 px groove texture: very cheap but a different design. Suggested: (1).

- **GPU-drawn map roads** (planned for 1.4.0, decided 2026-11-15; after issue #4, modelled on FactoryAtlas's map painter): replace the 512 m picture tiles (CPU-painted, 1 px = 1 m, ~1.3 MB each with mips: 130-260 MB for a network across much of the map, blurry at high zoom, whole tiles repainted on a change) with shapes drawn by the GPU, as Atlas does: one 16-32 byte record per road piece (~4 m strip: position, half length / width, angle, colour / style) in a Slate instance buffer (ISlateUpdatableInstanceBuffer) drawn with a map material, a few draw calls per frame; pan / zoom change only the transform. Intersections (outlines that do not fit one record) as triangles in a small separate buffer. Records sorted by height so bridges still cover what is under them. Estimates for ~4000 roads (~100 m each): ~100,000 records, ~3 MB, near-zero CPU per frame (Atlas: ~0.3 ms per million buildings), light GPU work even on a handheld iGPU; buffers freed while the map is closed and rebuilt on opening. Best fit for low-VRAM handhelds (shared memory). Keep: our layer directly above mMap (issue #4 rule), "Show Roads on Map", road colours / paint.
  - **Detail when zoomed in** (other map mods allow ~8-10x more zoom): above a zoom threshold (the material gets pixels per metre), fade in the lane lines (dividers, dashes 3 m / 6 m gap, continuous, side lines, in the road's line colours; kept at least ~1 px wide) and, higher still, the lane markings (arrows, numbers, vehicle icons) as small textured quads from one icon atlas, drawn at their real size and turned with the lane. Same records as the strips (lanes count, line style) plus one record per marking.
  - Stopgap if memory is reported before then: free the tiles while the map is closed and repaint only the visible ones on opening.

## On hold

Preferred: the text drawn with the game's font (engine fallback fonts for other scripts) into a texture at run time, cutting a quad on the road like the vehicle icons (drapes, line colour, glow, paint gun); one texture per distinct text; a rebindable key opens a text box, E edits a placed text. ## Later (1.5.0 or after)
- **Integration with other mods / Automated Machines** (discussed 2026-11-12, postponed): no hard link between mods; a
  name-based contract through Unreal reflection (works in the shipped game, where metadata is stripped). An actor is
  "automatable" when it has `AM_GetAlarms()`, `AM_GetActions()`, `AM_DoAction(Action, On)` and an `AM_OnAlarm(Alarm)`
  event; Automated Machines finds them by name and lists them like a machine's alarms / actions. Road Maker candidates:
  intersection "Vehicle enters / leaves", "Busy / Clear" (the detection traffic lights need), road "Vehicle on road /
  Road clear", boom barrier actions "Open / Closed" + alarms, traffic light "Turns green / red" + "Hold red". Optional
  extra: a Road Maker subsystem publishing world events (road built / dismantled / cut / split / merged / joined,
  intersection built) for other mods. Open questions: `AM_` naming as the shared standard; which vehicles count.
