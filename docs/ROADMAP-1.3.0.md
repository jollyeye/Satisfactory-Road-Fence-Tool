# Road Maker 1.3.0 roadmap

1.2.1 is released (2026-11-12). Work for the next version; the 1.2.0 roadmap (`ROADMAP-1.2.0.md`) is kept as history.
Status words: **DONE** (tested in game), **built, awaiting test**, otherwise to do.

## Done
- **Map road layer**: DONE in code (2026-11-12, `Private/Map/RMMapLayer.cpp`). Our canvas sits in the map's `Overlay_0` right after `BPW_MapAreaPainter` (the game pans / zooms it; icons stay on top). 512 m tiles at 1 px/m, painted at 2x and averaged (anti-aliasing), with mips and colour bleed at the edges; only tiles with roads exist. Roads are painted in 4 m pieces and intersections whole, all in height order, so overpasses and ramps cover what they pass over. Fill by look (cyberpunk = primary colour), side lines 1.5 m just inside the edge, only when on, in the secondary colour. Only while the map is open: changed pieces repaint their tiles; work spread over frames (3 ms building, 4 ms painting). Still to test: big save first open. **"Show Roads on Map"** (**DONE, tested 2026-11-13**): a row right above the game's "Show Stamps on Map" in the map side menu (BPW_MapMenu: text found by its English source; the stamps row is Overlay_2 > mShowStampsButton in CanvasPanel_0; ours is placed in that canvas one row higher and the list above (Widget_Scrollbox) is made one row shorter). Copies the stamps button style, text font (+1 pt) and uses the game's own BPW_OptionsCheckbox (state via SetChecked_NoNotify); grey 2 px line on top; button not focusable (IsFocusable by reflection) so the hover highlight clears. On by default, remembered (GameUserSettings.ini `[RoadMaker] bMapShowRoads`); off = layer hidden and nothing painted. The game's own icon filters still do not work (2026-11-13).
  - Bridge shadows: tried and dropped (2026-11-12): not worth the edge cases (joined roads, parallel roads); height order is enough. If ever wanted, the agreed definition: the centre lines cross (seen from above), 4 m+ clearance measured at the crossing point, intersections count by area and never cast, the 3 m band on both sides darkens only the crossed piece.
- **Intersection curbs like the vehicle paths** (2026-11-12): the roads' Bezier algorithm (MakeQuadratic) tried and rejected (odd S-curves, paths left the curb). Now trying curbs made with the same curve as the vehicle paths / rails through the junction (`MakeFixedEnds`, circular-arc tangents): in the 8 test layouts of `Tools/IconSource/intersection_curb_bezier.py` no lane path leaves the curb (the old curbs had 1 in a narrow Y). Simple curbs stay as fallback when the outline would cross itself. Every intersection changes a little on load (curbs slightly rounder): fences on curbs may need rebuilding. Keep only if it looks better in game. **DONE: kept (2026-11-12).** Also: side-by-side road ends (split roads, or roads that line up) count as one road slot per 4 foundations of width, one outline and one arrow in the pick overlay.

## Later (1.4.0 or after)
- **Sign wall** (planned for 1.4.0, decided 2026-11-13): a fence that shows a sign design repeated along its whole length, following its curve. Its own fence object, one side only (the road side), no material tab and no swatch / colour options: the design is its only look. First version 4 m tall, using the 4 m sign's size and layouts (read from the game's sign type at run time, so the proportions and the repeat length match); a 1 m tall wall with the 1 m sign later.
  How: the game's widget signs draw their design (FPrefabSignData) into a render target through AFGSignSubsystem and show it on a plane with a material instance (`AFGBuildableWidgetSign::SetupMaterialInstanceForProxyPlane` is virtual). The wall owns a hidden companion sign (subclass of AFGBuildableWidgetSign: no mesh, no collision, not in the build menu) standing inside the wall at the middle of its length, its significance range raised to half the wall's length plus the normal sign range so the design shows anywhere along it. The wall puts the companion's render target on its own mesh with UVs repeating every (height x the sign's aspect). Saved reference wall -> companion. E on the wall opens the game's sign editor for the companion; copy / paste settings and middle click copy the design; multiplayer and saving come from the game's sign. Dismantle takes the companion along (free, nothing to refund); Cut / Join give each new piece its own companion with a copy of the design. Check blueprints carry both.
  Limits: fixed sign sizes (the game's editor has no free proportions); the design is as sharp as the game's sign texture (soft right next to a tall wall); far away, like signs, no design (plain colour).
