ROAD MAKER 1.2.1 - TEST CHECKLIST
=================================
Riskiest first. Tick [x] as you go. If something fails, note the line and send FactoryGame.log
(C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Local\FactoryGame\Saved\Logs\FactoryGame.log).


1. SAVES AND LOADING (a bug here breaks people's worlds)
--------------------------------------------------------
[ ] Load a save made with 1.1.x: roads, intersections, fences and walls all appear; lines, paint and looks unchanged.
[ ] Load a 1.2.0 save with lane markings: they are on the road, right colour; cyberpunk ones glow.
[ ] Save, quit to menu, load again: markings still follow the road's curve (not flat) and keep their variant.
[ ] Markings painted before the "slices" update are still there (flat, but present).
[ ] Repainted markings keep their colour after reload.
[ ] Uneven intersections from an older save load with the new smooth top; roads still meet their mouths with no gap.
[ ] Paths / foundations / fences already built on an uneven intersection: note any that now float or sink (a few cm is expected).

--------------
[ ] Dismantle a road with markings, paths and tracks on it: all go together, refund correct.
[ ] Dismantle one marking alone: only that one goes, the road stays.
[ ] Mass dismantle (area select) over roads with markings: nothing left floating, no crash.
[ ] Dismantle the road under a stacked fence wall: the wall stays.

[ ] Without Ctrl: fences place freely on/near roads, no automatic road-edge placement.
[ ] Signs, NO Ctrl: on a fence wall face, a stacked wall, the gantry beam and columns. Flat, every 1 m (walls) / 2 m (gantry); scroll turns 45 deg.
[ ] Signs on normal ground / game walls: the game's own placement, with the leg.
[ ] Wall attachments (conveyor wall mount + hole, power and pipe mounts, ladder, fan, vent, beam connectors) on fence walls and the gantry.
[ ] Street lights + Ctrl: single road; between two roads side by side (NO flicker, faces the road you aim at); intersection curb. Scroll flips them.
[ ] Power poles + Ctrl on road edges.
[ ] Vehicle paths / railway tracks + Ctrl on a lane, also aiming right AT an arrow: one-click lane path.
[ ] Vehicle path + Ctrl on an intersection: all connections shown; on an uneven intersection they follow the surface (not through it); all green; vehicles drive them smoothly.
[ ] Railway track + Ctrl on an intersection: connections still shown and valid (old straight curve; note if they cut through an uneven top).
[ ] Foundations / ramps / frames near a road end: snap to the end in 4 m steps; scroll turns 90 deg.
[ ] Road + Ctrl aimed at a road: builds beside it. Aimed at a marking: same.
[ ] Sign Gantry aimed at a road with markings: goes across the road.
