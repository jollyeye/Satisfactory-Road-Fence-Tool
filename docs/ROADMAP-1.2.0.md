# Road Maker 1.2.0 roadmap

**1.2.1 released 2026-11-12** (fixes and improvements after 1.2.0, see the changelog in Tools/Release/ficsit-page.md; anything still found goes to 1.3.0).

**Status 2026-11-11: everything below is done and tested in game; release pass (version 1.2.0, changelog, steel pipe item = `Desc_SteelPipe_C` verified) done.** Items 8 and 11 moved to 1.3.0.

Agreed list (2026-11-11). Notes are first thoughts on how each could be done.
Tick items off and add findings as we go. Changes for the release go in the changelog in
`Tools/Release/ficsit-page.md` as usual.

| 1 | Better road curves | M | **Done (to test):** quadratic Bezier ported from the developer's 2D test (`CurveRoads`, `solveControlPoint`) into `RMGeometry::MakeQuadratic`: one quadratic for C-curves (control point = where the direction lines meet), two joined quadratics for S-curves, wide turns, U-turns. Free end heads along the chord, turned by the wheel. Old saves unaffected (stored as cubic points). |
| 2 | Tool: Cut Road | M | **Done (to test):** build menu category **Road Tools** (Builder tab, road glyph icon set at run time by `RMGameWorldModule::SetRoadToolsCategoryIcon`) > **Edit road** > **Cut Road** (own top tab tried and dropped: the build menu only lists building recipes; a tool is a recipe whose hologram edits instead of building) (`ARMRoadCutHologram`, stub buildable `ARMBuildableRoadTool`). Orange cross-section outline at the aimed point (red within 1 m of an end); click = `ARMBuildableRoad::CutAt`: two new roads refitted through each part of the curve (keys every 2 m, exact ends), same class / recipe / paint / lines; old road hidden and dismantled. Hologram `Construct` returns the first piece. Game rail tracks and vehicle paths on the road (its riders) are split where they cross the cut, with the game's own `AFGBuildableRailroadTrack::Split` / `AFGVehiclePathSegment::Split` (pieces stay connected); the old road's dismantle leaves them alone (`mBeingCut`). Fences: not cut yet. |
| 3 | Tool: Split Road | M | **Done (to test):** splits a road *lengthwise* (agreed with the developer): aim across a road 2+ wide, an orange line along it shows the split at whole foundations (every 8 m) from the left edge, click = `ARMBuildableRoad::SplitLengthwise`: two roads of the matching Build_Road_Nx classes / recipes (loaded by path), each the original curve moved sideways by `OffsetCurve`, same lines and paint. Width is a class value (`mWidthInFoundations`, not saved), so only whole widths. Game tracks / paths on the road are ignored (left as they are). Same hologram class as Cut Road (`mSplitTool`), `ARMBuildableSpline::SpawnLike` shared with cutting. |
| 4 | Tool: Adjust Curvature | M-L | **Done (to test):** build menu Road Tools > Edit road. `ARMRoadAdjustHologram` (derives from `ARMSplineHologram` for the scroll / hold (H) / nudge machinery; `UpdateCurve` and `GetPlacedPointAxis` made virtual). Step 0: orange see-through circles (procedural mesh, `M_RM_Overlay`) at road ends within 150 m, list refreshed twice a second; ends joined to an intersection are skipped, and ends where exactly two roads meet centre to centre (`JoinTolerance`) are grabbed together. Click = grab. Step 1: the joint follows the aim (+ road height), snaps to free road ends / intersection mouths when only one road moves, direction = chord (far end of A to far end of B, or to the joint) turned by the wheel; each road is refitted with `MakeQuadratic` from its unchanged far end to the joint; previews built like the road hologram (second mesh for road B); validity like a new road (length, turn radius, grade). Second click = `Construct`: `SpawnLike` new roads (class, recipe, paint, lines, cost scaled by length), old ones `DismantleReplaced` (tracks / paths on them are left alone). Grabbed roads are hidden while previewed (`SetGrabbedRoadsHidden`, restored in `Destroyed`; hiding keeps their collision, so nobody falls) and marked with a camera-facing wireframe (edges + a rib every 4 m). Curves use the Smooth height easing (far end keeps its slope, free joint level, snapped joint takes the slope of what it snaps to). Foundations: one road snaps to the nearest edge (like a new road), two roads to the 4 m grid on the top face. Not done: Straight build mode. |
| 5 | 4-wide road | S-M | **Done (to test):** `Road_4x` (32 m) with icon (`render_icons.py road4`), Road Lines "4 lanes, dashed / continuous" (icons drawn from the 3-lane ones), lane limit raised from 3 to 4 everywhere (markings, vanilla vehicle path lanes). Intersections take any width. |
| 6 | New road materials: concrete and cyberpunk (**built, awaiting in-game test**) | L | A **Roads** section in the game's Material tab (Asphalt / Concrete / Cyberpunk), switched in place like the fence walls. Cyberpunk = the matte plastic with circuit grooves and glowing lane lines from the concepts (`docs/concepts/`): surface takes the primary colour, lines the secondary. Concrete: foundation-style concrete atlas. Intersections too. |
| 7 | Road hologram snaps beside a built road | S-M | **Done (to test):** snap key + aim at a road = parallel of the whole road on the aimed side (`ARMRoadHologram::TrySnapBesideRoad`, `OffsetCurve` by both half widths), flush top, built with one click. No length / turn / grade rules; only inner edge radius >= half width. |
| 8 | Road preview on the map | - | **Moved to 1.3.0** (2026-11-11). |
| 9 | Highway sign gantry (**built, awaiting in-game test**) | M | A frame over the road (posts each side, beam across, sized to the road width) holding the game's signs, or our own sign panels. Snap to a road at the aimed point. Done: built from the game's H-beam mesh (SM_Beam_04, found with the RMBeamLog: a 1 m piece stretched along its Z): 2 H-beam columns on the road fence lines standing on upright connection cubes (SM_ConnectionCube_Wide_01), the cross beam on top of the columns in 3 pieces (1 m shelf beam SM_Beam_05 at each end, stretched H-beam between, because the shelf beam repeats its pattern instead of stretching), 1 m tall, ends flush with the columns, invisible box collision; a custom own-mesh gantry can come in 1.3.0. Also: 2-click placement (height by mouse, 2 m steps, min 8 m), steel beam + pipe cost per 8 m, game signs snap to the beam with the snap key (`ARMBuildableGantry`, `ARMGantryHologram`). |
| 10 | Road markings: arrows and numbers | M | **Built (to test):** customizer, Road Lines tab, new section **Markings**: Lane Arrow and Lane Number, sized to the lane (arrow head = half the lane width; 1-lane roads count 2 m lanes; capped at a 12 m lane = 1.5 foundations); the medium versions were dropped. Draped: cut into slices every ~50 cm that lie on the road (road surface sampled under it when painted and saved with it), so they follow slopes, banking and curves; a flat copy is drawn beyond 300 m and nothing beyond 600 m. Adjust Curvature moves the markings onto the reshaped road (same share of its length, nearest lane, own paint and look). Pick one, aim at a road: a local ghost shows it on the lane centre you aim at (lane centres follow the side lines and dividers; 1-lane roads every 2 m), facing the way you look along the road; mouse wheel (consumed by us while a marking is selected and the paint gun is aiming) picks the variant (6 arrows, digits 0-9); click paints (server spawns the buildable, multiplayer via RCO). Flat geometry (14 triangles for an 8), stretched 1.8x along the road. Takes the road's look and paint: asphalt / concrete show the primary colour (like the middle lines), cyberpunk glows in the secondary colour with the road's own line formula (custom colours may be negative, e.g. (-20, 5, -18.75) = saturated green); repainting with a swatch recolours it the same way; Material tab switches its look. Dismantled alone or with its road. Free; the Build_ / Recipe_ assets still exist (needed for dismantling) but are not in the build menu. Not yet: crosswalks, stop lines, intersections. Known: markings block the build gun's aim, so aim next to one to place a fence or gantry. |
| 11 | Traffic lights | - | **Moved to 1.3.0** (2026-11-11): they should track the intersection and detect whether the path is free, not just be for show. |

## Suggested order
Small and self-contained first, then the tools, then the big visual work:
5 (4-lane) → 7 (side snap) → 2 → 3 (slicer / splitter, shared code) → 1 (curves) → 4 (move ends)
→ 11 → 9 → 10 → 6 (materials) → 8 (map preview, research-heavy, can slip to 1.3.0).
